Connection security

How your connection is protected.

Your phone and computer lock the messages they send to each other using encryption. Shahi’s connection service passes those messages along without being able to read them.

What happens when I scan the code?
  1. The code lets your phone connect to your computer. It works once and expires after ten minutes.
  2. Your devices set up a protected connection. The connection service cannot read the secret in your code.
  3. Your computer remembers the connected device so it can recognize it next time.
  4. Remove a device’s access in Settings to disconnect it and stop it from connecting again.

Keep your connection code private. Anyone who uses it can control the work running on your computer.

What information can Shahi see?

Shahi’s connection service and its hosting provider, Cloudflare, can see connection information, such as your internet address, device identifiers, and when and how much data is sent. This helps diagnose connection problems and misuse. They cannot read the messages sent through this protected connection.

Your connected devices can read your messages, so keep them secure. The web app relies on software delivered by getshahi.dev. If you turn on notifications, notification providers receive their contents separately. Encryption cannot protect a device that someone has broken into or prevent connection outages.

Shahi has internal security reviews, but has not been independently certified. The security guide below explains the encryption in detail.

Setup

Install. Scan. Connect.

First install herdr, the app that keeps your AI assistants running, on your Mac or Linux computer. Then paste these two lines into its terminal (the window where you type commands). Open Shahi on your phone and scan the code that appears.

Step-by-step setup guide ↗
On your computer
herdr plugin install iYassr/shahi
herdr plugin action invoke shahi.pair

Each code can be used once and expires after 10 minutes.

Advanced: already use SSH to access your computer? You can use that connection in the mobile app. Connection options
