Setup

Install. Scan. Connect.

Two commands in herdr on your Mac or Linux computer, then one scan on your phone: about a minute once herdr is running.

On your phone, open getshahi.dev/pwa and tap Scan QR code, or scan it with the iOS app. Your camera app can’t pass this code to the browser, so scan it from inside Shahi.

New to herdr? Shahi shows the agents you run inside it. Install herdr first, then start your agents there.

Step-by-step setup guide ↗
On your computer, in herdr
herdr plugin install iYassr/shahi
herdr plugin action invoke shahi.pair

Needs herdr 0.9.0 or newer, git, and bun 1.3.13 or newer (if bun is missing, the install adds it), on macOS or on Linux with systemd. Each code can be used once and expires after 10 minutes.

What Shahi does

01

Encrypted end to end, and live

Your phone and computer encrypt every message they send each other. Shahi’s relay passes them along without being able to read them, so there is no Shahi account, no VPN and no open port. Conversations update as the agent writes.

02

Like WhatsApp, for your agents

Messages, tool calls, file changes and results arrive as a conversation made for your phone. Permission prompts Shahi recognises from Claude Code and Codex become buttons. Reply in a few taps and watch the agent carry on.

03

Built on herdr

herdr is a lightweight home for your agents, with spaces, panes and live status for every one. Shahi mirrors all of it, so you can browse your spaces and start new agents from your phone.

Why not a terminal app?

A terminal on a phone is not a good way to talk to an agent.

SSH apps give you a terminal wrapped for a wide screen: you pinch, scroll sideways and hunt for Esc and Tab. Shahi reads the transcripts that Claude Code, Codex and Cursor keep for themselves, so their work fits your screen. The terminal is still one tap away, on the Screen tab, for the moments you need it and for any other agent herdr runs.

Connection security

How your connection is protected.

Your phone and computer lock the messages they send to each other using encryption. Shahi’s relay, a connection service on Cloudflare, passes those messages along without being able to read them.

What happens when I scan the code?
  1. The code lets your phone connect to your computer. It works once and expires after ten minutes.
  2. Your devices set up a protected connection. The relay cannot read the secret in your code.
  3. Your computer remembers the connected device so it can recognize it next time.
  4. Remove a device’s access in Settings to disconnect it and stop it from connecting again.

Keep your connection code private. Anyone who uses it can control the work running on your computer.

What information can Shahi see?

Shahi’s relay and its hosting provider, Cloudflare, can see connection information, such as your internet address, device identifiers, and when and how much data is sent. This helps diagnose connection problems and misuse. They cannot read the messages sent through this protected connection.

Your connected devices can read your messages, so keep them secure. The web app runs code from getshahi.dev, published by the same developer who runs the relay. A malicious release or a compromised website could read an open session or a remembered pairing, because encryption shields your messages from the relay, not from the app’s own code. The iPhone app comes through TestFlight and signed updates instead, and it can skip the relay by using your own SSH.

If you turn on iPhone notifications, Expo and Apple can read each one: the space name, the terminal title or pane name, the pane to open and your computer’s public identifier. Browser notifications are encrypted for your browser alone. Encryption cannot protect a device that someone has broken into or prevent connection outages.

Shahi’s security has been reviewed internally but has not had an independent audit. The security guide explains the encryption in detail.

Prefer no third party in the path? The iPhone app (beta) can connect over your own SSH instead. To stop your computer using the relay at all, add RELAY_URL= to the plugin’s .env and restart Shahi. QR pairing and the web app then stop working, and you connect over SSH.
