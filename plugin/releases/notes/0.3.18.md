### Reader follows a conversation sent to the background

When a Claude Code conversation is sent to the background, Claude continues it in a new session while the terminal's own process keeps the old one. Reader followed the old session and showed its last reply, hours out of date, while Screen showed the live conversation. Reader now follows Claude's record of where the conversation continued.

### Reader shows the conversation a backgrounded pane shows

A terminal whose Claude parked its conversation as a background job keeps showing that job. herdr could report another background job for the pane, so Reader showed a different conversation from Screen. Reader now reads the job Claude's own record says the pane shows.

### Messages typed while Claude was working

A message typed while Claude was busy reached Claude but never appeared in Reader. It does now, as your message. Background reports delivered the same way, including a monitor's events, appear as notices rather than as messages from you, in Claude Code and in Codex.

### Tasks and subagents in Reader

Reader shows Claude Code's task list as it stands, read from Claude's own task store, and each subagent as its own row with its state and report. A background subagent's report joins its row instead of appearing as a message from you.

### Colour on the Screen tab

Use iPhone TestFlight 1.0.0 (31) for the matching app: the Screen tab draws the terminal in its colours, and Reader shows the task list and subagents.

### Compatibility

Supports herdr 0.9.0 through 0.9.3. Existing pairings, API 5 clients and encrypted transport 2 remain supported; older apps keep their current Reader and plain Screen.
