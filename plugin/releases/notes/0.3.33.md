### Codex conversations stay readable while Codex asks

While Codex 0.163 waits for an answer, it puts "[ ! ] Action Required" at the front of its terminal title and blinks it once a second. Shahi showed that as the conversation's name, and for a Codex you started yourself in a terminal, Reader lost the conversation for as long as the question was open, because it finds that conversation by its name. Shahi now ignores Codex's status in the title: the conversation keeps its name and stays readable while you answer its question.

### Compatibility

Supports herdr 0.9.0 and newer on protocol 22 (tested with 0.9.0 through 0.9.5), API 5 and encrypted transport 2. Checked against Claude Code 2.1.304, Codex 0.163.0, OpenCode 1.19.3 and Antigravity 1.4.2. Existing pairings and supported older apps remain compatible. No new app version is needed.
